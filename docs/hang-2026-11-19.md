# The phone hangs of 2026-11-18/19: firmware memory in the kernel's memory map

Status: **fixed** by [`kernel/0011`](../patches/kernel/0011-oneplus6-reserved-memory-as-mainline.patch) (device
tree only), in the default build since 2026-11-19. This is the record of how the cause was found, with the tools
that found it. Times are US Pacific.

## Summary

- **Symptom.** Under OH 7.0 the phone froze (screen frozen or black, USB gone) and ended in Qualcomm crash-dump mode
  (USB `05c6:900e`), where it waits until it is restarted. It happened every few hours in corpus rounds (1000 apps
  launched one after another), within a minute of a storage or memory stress, once during a `dd` of the system
  image to `system_b` and once while the phone booted. There was no kernel panic and no watchdog bite; the screen's
  console (`loglevel=1`) and ramoops had nothing.
- **Cause.** The port's OnePlus 6 device tree (`sdm845-oneplus-common.dtsi` of its 5.10 kernel) reserves 2 MB at
  0x88f00000 (`removed_region`), where mainline reserves 28 MB. The 26 MB at **0x89100000-0x8aafffff** are
  protected by the firmware, but the kernel listed them as System RAM and its page allocator handed their pages out.
  An access to such a page never completes, and within seconds the whole phone stops. When that happened depended on
  memory pressure, which decides when the allocator reaches those pages: page cache under storage I/O, tmpfs, large
  app launches, a boot. Hence hangs that looked random and followed load.
- **Proof.** On the unfixed kernel, a single 4-byte read of 0x89100000 through the kernel's linear map
  ([`guardprobe.ko`](../patches/staging/hang/guardprobe)) froze the phone into crash-dump mode.
- **Fix.** `kernel/0011` reserves memory as mainline does: `removed_region` 28 MB (up to qseecom at 0x8ab00000), the
  4 KiB guard page on each side of rmtfs (0xf5b00000, 0xf5d01000), and the bootloader's splash framebuffer
  (`cont_splash_mem`, 36 MB at 0x9d400000). Only the DTB changes; the kernel image and its modules stay the same.
- **Check.** With the new DTB, [`mem-stress.sh 4 10`](../patches/staging/hang/mem-stress.sh) ran its 10 minutes
  (193 fills of a 4 GB tmpfs) and [`io-stress.sh 15`](../patches/staging/hang/io-stress.sh) its 15 minutes
  (34 rounds, about 100 GB written); with the old DTB each had hung the phone within 30-40 s. `/proc/iomem` no longer
  lists 0x89100000-0x8aafffff as RAM and shows the guard pages and the splash region reserved. The phone's `boot_b`
  then got that boot image (the installed kernel, ramdisk and command line with the new DTB;
  [`scripts/test-kernel.sh`](../scripts/test-kernel.sh) with `DTB_ONLY=1` and `kernel/0011` rebuilds it byte for
  byte from the installed image), and the corpus round resumed on it.
- The 6.1 branch (`main`) builds the same kernel and device tree and carries the same patch.

## How it was narrowed down

1. **No evidence at first.** The command line has `loglevel=1` (a flood of kernel errors drawn on the screen's
   console had frozen the phone, see `BOOT_CMDLINE_BASE` in `scripts/config.sh`), so the console zone of ramoops
   kept nothing useful, and ramoops records do not survive a restart on this phone anyway.
2. **The RAM.** Crash-dump mode keeps RAM as it was. The main branch's `scripts/crashdump.py kmsg` reads the
   kernel's own log buffer (every message, whatever the loglevel), all tasks with the stacks of running and blocked
   ones, and TrustZone's and the AOP's diagnostics over USB; its `scripts/recover-phone.sh` captures, restarts the
   phone and waits for OH, and the round's supervisor (`westlake/tools/supervise-round.sh`) calls it after every
   hang. How to read such a capture, and what TrustZone's counters mean, is in the main branch's
   [`docs/crash-dump.md`](https://github.com/a2hlab/westlake-oneplus/blob/main/docs/crash-dump.md).
3. **15:14, the first capture.** The last kernel records were the audio DSP's fatal error ("SFR Init: wdog or
   kernel error suspected": the text the DSP firmware writes at boot, so the DSP died without writing a reason),
   "crash detected in remoteproc-adsp", the start of its recovery, and nothing after. TrustZone's log, put on the
   kernel's clock, had bus (NoC) error events 53 ms *before* the DSP's report, and its counters had NoC error #186
   three times. The DSP's crash was part of the event, not its start. Its automatic restart was turned off anyway
   (`westlake/03-prep.sh` on main: this 5.10 kernel's restart path is not safe), and the phone hung again at 16:04.
4. **A clean panic as the baseline.** A deliberate panic showed which of TrustZone's counters come with every entry
   into crash-dump mode (table below): NoC error #226 once, SGIReset, SGIFatal and a reset entry of type 0x33. Only
   #186 was particular to the hangs (15:14 and 17:10).
5. **Storage I/O?** `io-stress.sh` alone hung the phone in about 40 s, with no app running, and the
   capture showed the UFS host no longer completing commands. But neither UFS interconnect votes (as 6.x has them;
   staged `0007`) nor UFS power management turned off kept it from hanging.
6. **Memory.** `mem-stress.sh` writes only to a tmpfs, no storage at all, and hung the phone as quickly. So the
   trigger was touching freshly allocated memory, and the stuck UFS commands were a consequence: with a CPU frozen in
   a memory access, the bus and the UFS host's transfers stall too.
7. **The memory map.** Against the sdm845-mainline tree (codeberg.org/sdm845/linux, `sdm845-next`), the port's
   OnePlus 6 reserved-memory nodes differ in exactly three places:

   | region | port (5.10) | mainline |
   |---|---|---|
   | `removed_region` at 0x88f00000 | 2 MB | 28 MB (0x1c00000), up to qseecom at 0x8ab00000 |
   | rmtfs guard pages | not reserved: rmtfs is 0xf5b01000 + 2 MB | 0xf5b00000 + 0x202000 with `qcom,use-guard-pages` (a 4 KiB page on each side), which this kernel's rmtfs driver lacks |
   | `cont_splash_mem` | not reserved | 0x9d400000, 36 MB: the bootloader's framebuffer, scanned out until the display driver takes over |

   Both trees have the same comment above `removed_region`: reserving it "is also needed to prevent random crashes
   which are most likely modem related".
8. **The proof.** On the installed kernel `/proc/iomem` listed 89100000-8aafffff as System RAM, and so did the
   guard pages and the splash region. `guardprobe.ko addr=0x89100000` logged "reading 0x89100000 through the linear
   map" and never "read completed": `insmod` was still the current task on CPU3 in the capture. Within a second the
   UFS host stopped answering (a power-mode command timed out, the host reset failed); apart from SELinux audit
   lines those are the last records, about 5 s before the log ends.
9. **The fix and its check:** the mainline map (`kernel/0011`), RAM-booted with the installed kernel, then the two
   stresses (Summary).

## Tools

All of them stay useful for any change to the memory map or for another SDM845 phone.

- [`patches/staging/hang/mem-stress.sh`](../patches/staging/hang/mem-stress.sh) and
  [`io-stress.sh`](../patches/staging/hang/io-stress.sh): stresses that run on the phone (memory alone; storage
  I/O and its page cache). Before 0011 each hung the phone within a minute: a long run of both is the regression
  check for the memory map.
- [`patches/staging/hang/guardprobe`](../patches/staging/hang/guardprobe): a kernel module that reads one word of a
  physical page through the kernel's linear map, the way the page allocator's users touch memory. A read that never
  completes (the phone ends in crash-dump mode, the capture's log has "reading ..." without "read completed") is
  memory the firmware protects. It refuses addresses the kernel does not map as RAM, so on a kernel with the right
  map, `addr=0x89100000` reports "not in the kernel's memory map" without a read. Candidates: "System RAM" ranges
  of `/proc/iomem` that a newer device tree reserves. `build.sh` builds it for the kernel of `04-build-kernel.sh`.
- [`scripts/test-kernel.sh`](../scripts/test-kernel.sh): a kernel, or only a DTB, built with the phone's kernel
  configuration and RAM-booted with the installed ramdisk, so that kernel and device-tree changes can be tried
  without new images, modules or flashing. That is how the test kernel with the staged `0005`-`0007` and the
  memory-map DTB ran.
- RAM capture: the main branch's `scripts/crashdump.py` and `scripts/recover-phone.sh`
  ([`docs/crash-dump.md`](https://github.com/a2hlab/westlake-oneplus/blob/main/docs/crash-dump.md)).

### TrustZone's counters in the captures

`crashdump.py` writes TrustZone's diagnostic area to `ram/tzdiag.txt`: per-CPU reset entries and interrupt counts
per CPU. The deliberate panic shows what every entry into crash-dump mode leaves:

| capture | NoC #226 | SGIReset (#15) | SGIFatal (#13) and reset type 0x33 | NoC #186 | task on the SGIFatal CPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16:44 deliberate panic | CPU1 | CPUs 0, 1, 3-7 | CPU2 | - | `sh` (the one that wrote `c` to sysrq-trigger) |
| 15:14 round | CPU1 | CPU2 | CPU1 | 3 (CPU0 1, CPU5 2) | - (no task walk yet) |
| 17:10 `io-stress.sh` | CPU0 | CPUs 0, 3 | CPU1 | 7 (CPU7) | `dd` |
| 17:20 `io-stress.sh`, test kernel with `0005`-`0007` | CPU0 | CPUs 0, 1, 3, 4 | CPU2 | - | - (no task walk for the test kernel) |
| 17:28 `io-stress.sh`, UFS power management off | CPU0 | CPUs 0, 3 | CPU4 | - | `dd` |
| 17:35 `mem-stress.sh` | CPU0 | CPUs 0, 3 | CPU6 | - | `dd` |
| 19:08 `guardprobe` read of 0x89100000 | CPU0 | CPU0 | CPU3 | - | `insmod` |

- NoC error #226 once, SGIReset, SGIFatal and a reset entry of type 0x33 are in every capture, the panic's
  included: they belong to entering crash-dump mode and say nothing about the cause. Which CPU has SGIFatal and the
  reset entry does: in every capture with a task walk it was the CPU that panicked, or the one whose task was stuck
  in a memory access (`dd` allocating or writing pages, the probe's `insmod`).
- NoC error #186 is a real bus error, but only two hangs had it (15:14 with the audio DSP failing, 17:10 with the
  UFS host's transfers stuck); the other storage stresses, the memory stress and the probe had none. It can follow
  this fault; it is not its signature.
- Non-secure watchdog bites, XPU violations and the RPM watchdog stayed at zero in all of them.

## What else came out of it

- Staged in [`patches/staging/hang`](../patches/staging/hang): `0005` (the access-token device stops logging
  every terminal probe from services and apps: 39 % of the kernel log in a round) and `0006` (fastrpc session 0,
  from later 5.10 stable releases) remain optional improvements; `0007` (UFS interconnect votes) is not needed.
  `log_buf_len=4M` (also staged) makes the kernel's log ring 4 MB instead of 512 KB, about ten minutes of a round,
  for a capture.
- The audio and compute DSPs are not restarted after a crash (`westlake/03-prep.sh` on main): this kernel's
  SLIMbus driver lacks the restart handling of later kernels. The round's `westlake/tools/kmsg-watch.sh` reboots the
  phone after such a crash instead.
- A boot image made before 0011 keeps the old map, and `scripts/fetch-images.sh` keeps the kernel and DTB of the
  boot image it starts from. `TEST_BOOT_IMG=<that image> DTB_ONLY=1 scripts/test-kernel.sh OUT_DIR`, on a tree with
  0011 applied, gives such an image the new DTB.
